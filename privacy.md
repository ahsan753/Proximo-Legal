# Proximo — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 22 May 2026**
**Effective date: 22 May 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Proximo collects, uses, shares, and protects your personal data when you use the Proximo mobile application ("Proximo" or the "App"). Please read it alongside our Terms & Conditions.

## 1. Who is responsible for your data

Proximo is operated by Ahsan Shah, an individual developer, who is the data controller responsible for your personal data. For any privacy question or request, contact us at support@theproximoapp.com.

## 2. Who can use Proximo

Proximo is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a person under 18 has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

## 3. The personal data we collect

### Account and profile data

- Your email address, if you register with email.
- An identifier from Apple or Google if you use Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In. Where you use Apple's "Hide My Email" feature, we receive Apple's private relay address rather than your real email address.
- Your username and, optionally, a profile photo (avatar).
- Your interests — the categories you select.
- A home location and a preferred feed radius, if you set them.

### Location data

- With your permission, your device's location (via iOS Location Services). We use this to show you posts near you and to attach a location to posts you create. You can change or withdraw location permission at any time in iOS Settings (see section 7).

### Content you create

- Posts you create, including titles, descriptions, photos, categories, expiry times, the poster type (business or individual), any event capacity, and the location and location name attached to each post.
- Your RSVPs to events, and the attendee lists of events you host.
- Posts you save.
- Reports you submit about other users' content.

### Device and technical data

- A push notification token, so that we can deliver notifications to your device.
- Basic technical information needed to operate the App, such as your device type and operating system version.

We do **not** collect payment information, and Proximo does not contain in-app purchases.

## 4. How we collect your data

We collect data directly from you (when you register, set up your profile, or create content); automatically from your device (such as your location, with your permission, and your push notification token); and from Apple or Google when you choose to sign in using their services.

## 5. How we use your data, and our legal basis

We use your personal data to:

- create and manage your account and profile;
- show you a feed of nearby posts based on your location and chosen radius;
- let you create, display, and manage posts and RSVP events;
- send you push notifications about nearby posts that match your interests and about activity on your events (you can control these — see section 7);
- operate the reporting and moderation system and help keep the community safe;
- maintain the security and proper functioning of the App; and
- comply with our legal obligations.

We process your data on the basis of your consent (for example, for location access and push notifications), to perform our agreement with you under the Terms & Conditions (in order to provide the Service), for our legitimate interest in operating and securing the App, and to comply with the law. This is consistent with Qatar's Law No. 13 of 2016 Concerning Personal Data Privacy Protection.

## 6. Posts are public — what other users can see

Proximo is a public local feed. When you create a post, the following is visible to other users whose feed radius includes the post's location: the post's title, description, photo, category, expiry, the location and location name you attach, and your username and profile picture. Other users can also see the attendee list of RSVP events. Reports you submit are not shown to other users.

Please think carefully before posting, and avoid attaching a precise location — such as your exact home address — if you do not want it shared publicly.

## 7. Location and notifications — your controls

**Location.** Proximo asks for permission to use your device location so that it can show posts near you. You can grant, change, or withdraw this permission at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Proximo. If you turn location off, the core nearby-feed feature will not work, although you can still set a home location manually.

**Push notifications.** You can turn push notifications on or off at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications → Proximo. In-app notifications about activity on your events will still appear within the App.

## 8. Who we share your data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only in the following ways:

- **With other users** — the public post and profile information described in section 6.
- **With our service providers (sub-processors)** who help us operate Proximo:
  - **Supabase** — our backend, database, file storage, and authentication provider. Your account data, posts, and images are stored on Supabase's infrastructure.
  - **Apple** — for Sign in with Apple, and for delivering push notifications through the Apple Push Notification service.
  - **Google** — for Google Sign-In, if you choose to use it.
- **For legal reasons** — where required by law, regulation, legal process, or a governmental authority, or where necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of our users, the public, or us.

## 9. Where your data is stored, and international transfers

Our backend provider, Supabase, hosts Proximo's data in a data centre in Frankfurt, Germany, within the European Union. This means that if you use Proximo from Qatar or elsewhere, your personal data is transferred to and stored in the European Union. By using Proximo, you understand that your data will be processed in the locations described in this policy.

## 10. How long we keep your data

- **Posts** automatically expire after the period set by their author and are removed from the feed. Expired posts and their images may be retained for a short period before permanent deletion.
- **Account and profile data** is kept while your account remains active.
- **Reports** are kept for as long as needed to handle moderation and safety, including after the reported post has expired.
- When you **delete your account**, your profile, posts, saves, RSVPs, and device tokens are deleted. Limited information may remain for a reasonable period in backups, or for longer where we are required to keep it by law or for legitimate safety reasons.

## 11. How we protect your data

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including access controls and database-level security rules that restrict each user's data to that user. No system is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 12. Your rights

You have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you;
- **correct** data that is inaccurate or incomplete;
- **delete** your data and your account;
- **object to or restrict** certain processing of your data;
- **withdraw consent** you have previously given (such as for location access or notifications); and
- **complain** to the relevant data protection authority.

You can exercise many of these rights directly within the App: you can edit your profile, change your interests and radius, and delete your account. For any other request, contact us at support@theproximoapp.com and we will respond within a reasonable time.

If you are in Qatar and have a concern we are unable to resolve, you may contact the Compliance and Data Protection Department of Qatar's National Cyber Security Agency, which oversees Law No. 13 of 2016.

## 13. Deleting your account

You can delete your account from within Proximo. Deleting your account removes your profile and the content described in section 10. Some data may remain briefly in backups, or be retained where the law requires.

## 14. Third-party services and links

Proximo uses Apple, Google, and Supabase as described above, and uses Apple Maps (MapKit) to display posts on a map. These third parties handle data under their own privacy policies, which we encourage you to read. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third parties.

## 15. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest revision. If we make material changes, we will take reasonable steps to notify you through the App. Continuing to use Proximo after changes take effect means that you accept the updated policy.

## 2. About Proximo

Proximo is a hyperlocal, chronological events feed for iPhone. It lets you see posts about events, deals, gatherings, and activities happening near your current location, and to create your own posts. Posts are shown in time order, filtered by a distance radius you choose, and automatically expire after a period set by the person who created them.

Proximo is intended for casual, real-world community discovery. It is not a ticketing platform, a payment service, or a private messaging service.
